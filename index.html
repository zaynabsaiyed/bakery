FAQ

What makes your cookies special?

I use premium ingredients, including a carefully chosen flour blend and European-style butter that I brown for a rich, nutty flavor. Each flavor is thoughtfully made with its own mix-ins and finishing touches for a rich, satisfying bite.

How does ordering work?

Orders are open Monday through Thursday at 11:59 PM, or while supplies last, for pickup that Sunday. Choose your treats, enter your details, and pay through Zelle to complete your order.

Pickup is Sunday from 2–3 PM in the parking lot next to Grand Vista Pool. All orders are collected during this one-hour time window.

Why is pickup on Sunday if orders close on Thursday?

I bake everything in small batches in my little home oven. Closing orders on Thursday gives me time to plan ingredients, prepare everyone’s orders, and let my cookie dough chill overnight for a richer flavor. I bake close to pickup day so your treats are fresh and ready to enjoy on Sunday.

Cancellation and refund policy

Orders must be collected on the scheduled pickup date between 2–3 PM.

Each order is baked especially for you. All sales are final. Customer cancellations and missed pickups are non-refundable. If Good Flour Bakery is unable to fulfill your order, we will issue a full refund.
